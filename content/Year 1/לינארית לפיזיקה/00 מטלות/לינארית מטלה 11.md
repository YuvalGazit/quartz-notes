## ב
$$
\begin{matrix} 
||u||^{2}
=\langle u | u \rangle \\
=\langle \alpha v_{1}+\beta v_{2}+\gamma v_{3} | \alpha v_{1}+\beta v_{2}+\gamma v_{3} \rangle \\
=\alpha\langle \alpha v_{1}+\beta v_{2}+\gamma v_{3} | v_{1}\rangle +\beta\langle \alpha v_{1}+\beta v_{2}+\gamma v_{3} | v_{2}\rangle  +\gamma\langle \alpha v_{1}+\beta v_{2}+\gamma v_{3} | v_{3}\rangle   \\
=|\alpha|^{2}+|\beta|^{2}+|\gamma|^{2}
\end{matrix}
$$
## ג
$$
\begin{matrix} 
\langle u | T(u) \rangle  \\
=\langle \alpha v_{1}+\beta v_{2}+\gamma v_{3} | T(\alpha v_{1})+T(\beta v_{2})+T(\gamma v_{3}) \rangle \\
=\langle \alpha v_{1}+\beta v_{2}+\gamma v_{3} | \lambda_{1}\alpha v_{1}+\lambda_{2}\beta v_{2}+\lambda_{3}\gamma v_{3}\rangle  
\end{matrix}
$$
מסיימים בדומה להוכחה בסעיף הקודם:
$$
=\lambda_{1}|\alpha|^{2}+\lambda_{2}|\beta|^{2}+\lambda_{3}|\gamma|^{2}
$$
