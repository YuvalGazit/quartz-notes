## Cheatsheet
```dataview
table 
		type AS סוג, 
		looks AS מראה, 
		sort(environment) AS "סביבת היווצרות",
		sort(minerals) AS הרכב,
		list("**פלדה: **"+scratch_by_steel, "**ציפורן: **"+scratch_by_nail, "**חומצה: **"+acid_reaction) AS תכונות
FROM #Rock 
WHERE file.path!="YYY Templates/Rock.md"
SORT type ASC, environment ASC, file.name ASC
```
