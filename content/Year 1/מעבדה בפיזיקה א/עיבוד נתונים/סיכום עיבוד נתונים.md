# נוסחאות עיקריות
 
$$
\begin{matrix} 
(25) && \Delta\%=\frac{\Delta x}{|x|} \\ \\
(27) && \sigma = \frac{res}{\sqrt{ 12 }}\\ \\
(28) && \Delta x_{inst}=\frac{res}{\sqrt{ 12 }} \\ \\
(29) && \bar{x}=\frac{1}{N}\Sigma^N_{i=1}x_{i} \\ \\
(32) && s=\sqrt{ \frac{1}{N-1}\Sigma^N_{i=1}(x_{i}-\bar{x})^2 } \\ \\
(34)  && \Delta x_{stat}=\frac{s}{\sqrt{ N }} \\ \\
(35) && \Delta x =\sqrt{ \Delta x^{2}_{stat}+\Delta x^{2}_{inst} } \\  \\
(43) && \Delta y=\sqrt{ \sum_{i}\left( \frac{dy}{dx}\Delta x_{i} \right)^{2} } \\ \\
(50) && \chi^{2}_{red}=\frac{\chi^{2}}{N-K} \\  \\
(51) && N_{\sigma}=\frac{|x_{1}-x_{2}|}{\sqrt{ \Delta x_{1}^{2}+\Delta x_{2}^{2} }}
\end{matrix}
$$
